HOW TO PLAY

Identify the mystery firearm

You have 6 guesses to identify today's hidden firearm. Pick a manufacturer, then a model, and hit FIRE. Every guess reveals how close you are — across seven attributes.

Color key
PISTOL Exact match — the answer's value is identical.
9x19mm Close family — wrong, but in the same group (e.g., another 9mm-class pistol cartridge).
1985 No match — and for numbers, an arrow shows direction: ↑ answer is higher, ↓ answer is lower.
A few rules
  • One puzzle per day. Same gun for every player worldwide.
  • The puzzle resets at your local midnight.
  • Your progress is saved if you refresh — pick up where you left off.
  • Share your result with friends after you finish.
